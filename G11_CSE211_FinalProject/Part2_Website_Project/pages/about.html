About EventX

Welcome to EventX, the premier dynamic platform designed to simplify event planning and management for everyone.

Our Story

Founded in 2025, EventX was born out of a vision to bridge the gap between event organizers and attendees. We provide tools for budgeting, registration, and discovery to ensure every event is a success.

The Founder

EventX was established by Saif Amer, a visionary dedicated to improving digital planning experiences. Under his leadership, the platform has grown into a hub for community gatherings, tech summits, and educational workshops.

Our Mission

  • To provide a seamless registration experience.
  • To offer transparent budgeting tools for organizers.
  • To foster community growth through shared experiences.
